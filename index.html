About PUZZLIX

PUZZLIX is a browser-based puzzle platform built around simple controls, thoughtful challenges, and accessible gameplay.

Every puzzle runs entirely inside your browser. There is no server, no account and no tracking. Your progress, favourites and settings are stored locally on your own device.
